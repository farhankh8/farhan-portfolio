Privacy

What this site does with your information

Nothing is tracked. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, no advertising scripts and no session recording. There is no tag manager and no third party pixel on any page.

The contact form. If you use the form, your name, email address and message are sent to Web3Forms, which forwards them to the inbox behind this site so I can reply. Web3Forms processes the submission on my behalf. Nothing you type is stored on this website and nothing is added to a mailing list.

Web fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google. If you would rather avoid that, a content blocker will prevent the request and the page falls back to a system font.

Links to other sites. Pages and repositories on this site are hosted elsewhere. Their own privacy practices apply once you leave.

Asking me to remove something. If you would like something deleted from my inbox, email khmohammadfarhan.job@gmail.com and I will do it.
